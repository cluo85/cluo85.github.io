Beijing Star Halo Technology Co., Ltd ("we") operates the mobile game Galaxy Tycoon. Please read this policy before using the game.

1. Information we collect and how we use it

1.1 Providing the game

Your progress, settings, items and currency are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded to our servers. The game has no accounts, registration, login, nickname, avatar, friend list or chat features.

1.2 Advertising

The game shows interstitial and rewarded video ads through a third-party advertising SDK. The ad SDK collects device information on its own for ad delivery, measurement, attribution and fraud prevention. Personalized ads require your separate consent; you can turn them off at any time in Settings > Privacy & Data, after which we only request non-personalized ads.

1.3 Service security

We record crash logs and exception information to keep the service stable and to prevent cheating.

1.4 What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, phone number, email address, ID number, contacts, messages, call history, photos, videos, microphone or camera content, location, or clipboard content.

4. Sharing, transfer and public disclosure

We share device information with the SDKs above only as needed for the purposes described in sections 1 and 3. We do not transfer your personal information to any other company, organization or individual, and we do not publicly disclose it.

5. Cross-border transfer

The Pangle ads SDK used in this distribution stores data within the People's Republic of China; no cross-border transfer occurs. If that changes, we will obtain your separate consent as required by law.

6. Storage

Local data stays on your device and never leaves it. It remains there until you delete it in Settings > Privacy & Data or uninstall the game. Ad data is retained by the SDK under its own privacy policy. After the applicable period we delete or anonymize the information, unless the law requires otherwise.

7. How we protect your information

Local saves and settings are stored encrypted and readable only by the game. Access to personal information is limited to the minimum necessary personnel under confidentiality obligations. If a personal information security incident occurs, we will notify you and report to the regulator as required by law.

8. Your rights

You can view this policy, request a copy or correction, delete local data, and withdraw consent in Settings > Privacy & Data; withdrawal takes effect immediately without restarting the game. You can also turn off personalized ads, after which we only request non-personalized ads. The game has no accounts, so there is no account deletion process and we hold no data that can be linked to your identity. We respond to requests within 15 working days.

9. Minors

Users under 18 should read this policy and use the game under a guardian's guidance. For children under 14, we require verifiable guardian consent before processing personal information and do not knowingly collect it. Guardians may contact us to have such data deleted. The game has no accounts and no stranger-facing posting, messaging or voice chat.

10. Updates

When this policy changes we will notify you in-game and ask for your consent again. The current version is always available in Settings > Privacy & Data and at https://www.starhalobj.com/privacytap_en.html.
