Beijing Star Halo Technology Co., Ltd ("we") operates the mobile game Galaxy Tycoon. We treat your personal information with care and process it in accordance with applicable data protection laws. Please read this policy before using the game.

1. Information we collect and how we use it

1.1 Providing the game

Your progress, settings, items and currency are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded to our servers. The game has no accounts, registration, login, nickname, avatar, friend list or chat features.

1.2 Advertising

The game shows ads through a third-party advertising SDK, which collects device information and advertising identifiers on its own for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. On iOS, the advertising identifier (IDFA) is used only after you allow tracking in the system prompt and consent to personalized ads; you can turn tracking off at any time in system settings. Personalized ads require your separate consent and can be turned off in Settings > Privacy & Data.

1.3 Analytics

After you consent, we record events such as launches, level progress, ad views and purchases through a third-party analytics SDK to understand how features are used and to troubleshoot problems. We do not link analytics data to your identity.

1.4 Purchases and leaderboards

In the App Store version, purchases are processed by Apple; we never receive your payment account, card number or password. Leaderboards use Apple Game Center, which displays your Game Center nickname and score.

1.5 Information we do not actively collect

The game does not actively collect your name, phone number, email address, ID number, contacts, messages, call history, photos, videos, microphone or camera content, precise location, or clipboard content. If you email us, we process your email address and message only to respond to your request.

Purpose: in-app purchases and leaderboards

These SDKs may use randomly generated or system-provided device-related identifiers to distinguish devices; see their privacy policies for the specific types. We do not actively link these identifiers to your real-world identity. Such identifiers may still constitute personal information even if they do not display your name.

4. Sharing, transfer and public disclosure

The SDKs above process information as described in section 3 when providing their functions. Except for calls necessary to provide those functions, we do not actively provide your personal information to other third parties. We do not actively transfer or publicly disclose it, except where separate consent is obtained or applicable law provides otherwise. If a transfer is required by a merger, division or acquisition, we will identify the recipient and its contact details and require it to continue protecting the information. Leaderboards show your Game Center nickname and score only after you choose to submit a score.

5. Cross-border transfer

The servers of the SDKs above (Google, Apple) may be located outside your country or region and may involve cross-border transfers. We will identify overseas recipients, the purposes and methods of processing, the information types and the procedures for exercising rights, and will fulfil separate-consent and other applicable cross-border compliance duties.

6. Storage

Local saves are stored in the app's private storage and are not uploaded to our servers. They remain until you delete them in Settings > Privacy & Data or uninstall the game. Event-level Firebase Analytics data is retained for no longer than 14 months and then deleted automatically by Google; afterwards only aggregate statistics that cannot identify a single device remain. Ad data is handled by Google under its published privacy and retention policies. When information under our control is no longer necessary or its retention period expires, we delete or anonymize it as required by law, unless the law provides otherwise.

7. How we protect your information

Local saves are stored in the app's private storage directory and protected by integrity checks against tampering. We apply security measures appropriate to the purposes and risks of processing. If a personal information security incident occurs, we will take remedial action and fulfil reporting and notification duties as required by law.

8. Your rights

For information under our control that we can lawfully verify as relating to you, you may contact us under section 11 to request access, a copy, correction or supplementation. You can delete local saves, settings and consent records in Settings > Privacy & Data or by uninstalling the game. This does not directly delete data already lawfully processed by third-party SDKs; you may use the rights channels in their privacy policies or ask us for assistance. You can withdraw consent in Settings > Privacy & Data without restarting; this stops future consent-based collection but does not affect processing lawfully performed before withdrawal. You can turn off personalized ads, after which we only request non-personalized ads, and you can revoke tracking authorization in iOS system settings. The game has no accounts, so there is no account deletion process. We respond within the period required by law or, where none is specified, generally within 15 working days.

9. Minors

Users under 18 should read this policy with a guardian and use the game with the guardian's consent. Children under 14 should use the game only after obtaining guardian consent. We do not actively collect age information. A guardian who believes a child's information was processed without consent may contact us so we can handle it as required by law. The game has no accounts and no stranger-facing posting, messaging or voice chat.

We will ask for consent again where the purposes, methods or types of personal information processing change, or where applicable law otherwise requires renewed consent.
