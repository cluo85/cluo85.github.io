Beijing Star Halo Technology Co., Ltd ("we") operates the mobile game Galaxy Tycoon. We treat your personal information with care and process it in accordance with applicable data protection laws. Please read this policy before using the game.

1. Information we collect and how we use it

1.1 Providing the game

Your progress, settings, items and currency are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded to our servers. The game has no accounts, registration, login, nickname, avatar, friend list or chat features.

1.2 Advertising

The game shows ads through a third-party advertising SDK, which collects device information and advertising identifiers on its own for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. On iOS, the advertising identifier (IDFA) is used only after you allow tracking in the system prompt and consent to personalized ads; you can turn tracking off at any time in system settings. Personalized ads require your separate consent and can be turned off in Settings > Privacy & Data.

1.3 Analytics

After you consent, we record events such as launches, level progress, ad views and purchases through a third-party analytics SDK to understand how features are used and to troubleshoot problems. We do not link analytics data to your identity.

1.4 Purchases and leaderboards

In the App Store version, purchases are processed by Apple; we never receive your payment account, card number or password. Leaderboards use Apple Game Center, which displays your Game Center nickname and score.

1.5 Service security

We record crash logs and exception information to keep the service stable and to prevent cheating.

1.6 What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, phone number, email address, ID number, contacts, messages, call history, photos, videos, microphone or camera content, precise location, or clipboard content.

Provider: Google LLC

4. Sharing, transfer and public disclosure

We share information with the SDKs above only as needed for the purposes described in sections 1 and 3. We do not transfer your personal information to any other company, organization or individual, and we do not publicly disclose it. Leaderboards show your Game Center nickname and score only after you choose to submit a score.

5. Cross-border transfer

The servers of the SDKs above (Google, Apple) may be located outside your country or region. We transfer data only after informing you and obtaining consent as required by applicable law, and we require recipients to protect it to the same standard as this policy.

6. Storage

Local data stays on your device and never leaves it. It remains there until you delete it in Settings > Privacy & Data or uninstall the game. Event-level Firebase Analytics data is retained for no longer than 14 months and then deleted automatically by Google; afterwards only aggregate statistics that cannot identify a single device remain. Ad data is handled by Google under its published privacy and retention policies. After the applicable period we delete or anonymize the information, unless the law requires otherwise.

7. How we protect your information

Local saves and settings are stored encrypted and readable only by the game. Access to personal information is limited to the minimum necessary personnel under confidentiality obligations. If a personal information security incident occurs, we will notify you and report to the regulator as required by law.

8. Your rights

You can view this policy, request a copy or correction, delete local data, and withdraw consent in Settings > Privacy & Data; withdrawal takes effect immediately without restarting the game. You can turn off personalized ads, after which we only request non-personalized ads, and you can revoke tracking authorization in iOS system settings. The game has no accounts, so there is no account deletion process and we hold no data that can be linked to your identity. We respond to requests within 15 working days.

9. Minors

Users under 18 should read this policy and use the game under a guardian's guidance. For children under 14, we require verifiable guardian consent before processing personal information and do not knowingly collect it. Guardians may contact us to have such data deleted. The game has no accounts and no stranger-facing posting, messaging or voice chat.

10. Updates

When this policy changes we will notify you in-game and ask for your consent again. The current version is always available in Settings > Privacy & Data and at https://www.starhalobj.com/privacy.html.
