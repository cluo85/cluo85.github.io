Beijing Star Halo Technology Co., Ltd ("we") operates the mobile game Galaxy Tycoon. We treat your personal information with care and process it in accordance with the Personal Information Protection Law of the People's Republic of China and other applicable laws. Please read this policy before using the game.

1. Information we collect and how we use it

1.1 Providing the game

Your progress, settings, items and currency are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded to our servers. The game has no accounts, registration, login, nickname, avatar, friend list or chat features. This distribution offers no in-app purchases, leaderboards or analytics service.

1.2 Startup timing and information processed by the app

Before you accept this policy, the game shows a native privacy page and does not start the Unity engine runtime or initialize the Pangle Ads SDK. After consent, the app itself may read or process Android ID, OAID (Open Anonymous Device Identifier), device manufacturer and model, operating-system version, app package name and version, network state and IP address in order to recognize the installation, protect local saves, adapt to the device and handle basic diagnostics. Game saves are not uploaded, and these identifiers are not used to create a user account.

1.3 Advertising

The game shows interstitial and rewarded video ads through the Pangle Ads SDK. The SDK processes the device and network information listed in section 3 for ad delivery, measurement, attribution and fraud prevention. Personalized ads require your separate consent; you can turn them off at any time in Settings > Privacy & Data, after which we only request non-personalized ads.

1.4 Information we do not actively collect

The game does not actively collect your name, phone number, email address, ID number, contacts, messages, call history, photos, videos, microphone or camera content, precise location, or clipboard content. If you email us, we process your email address and message only to respond to your request.

4. Sharing, transfer and public disclosure

The SDK above processes information as described in section 3 when providing ads. Except for calls necessary to provide that function, we do not actively provide your personal information to other third parties. We do not actively transfer or publicly disclose it, except where separate consent is obtained or applicable law provides otherwise. If a transfer is required by a merger, division or acquisition, we will identify the recipient and its contact details and require it to continue protecting the information.

5. Cross-border transfer

This distribution does not include Firebase or other overseas analytics SDKs, and Unity Ads, Unity Analytics, Unity accounts and Unity payments are not enabled. The Unity engine runtime processes the information described in section 3 on your device for game operation, and we do not provide your personal information to overseas recipients ourselves. If a future feature requires an overseas transfer, we will provide separate notice and obtain separate consent as required by law.

6. Storage

Local saves are stored in the app's private storage and are not uploaded to our servers. They remain until you delete them in Settings > Privacy & Data or uninstall the game. Ad data is retained by the SDK under its own privacy policy. When information under our control is no longer necessary or its retention period expires, we delete or anonymize it as required by law, unless the law provides otherwise.

7. How we protect your information

Local saves are stored in the app's private storage directory and protected by integrity checks against tampering. We apply security measures appropriate to the purposes and risks of processing. If a personal information security incident occurs, we will take remedial action and fulfil reporting and notification duties as required by law.

8. Your rights

For information under our control that we can lawfully verify as relating to you, you may contact us under section 11 to request access, a copy, correction or supplementation. You can delete local saves, settings and consent records in Settings > Privacy & Data or by uninstalling the game. This does not directly delete data already lawfully processed by the SDK; you may use the rights channel in its privacy policy or ask us for assistance. You can withdraw consent in Settings > Privacy & Data without restarting; this stops future consent-based collection but does not affect processing lawfully performed before withdrawal. You can turn off personalized ads, after which we only request non-personalized ads. The game has no accounts, so there is no account deletion process. We respond within the period required by law or, where none is specified, generally within 15 working days.

9. Minors

Users under 18 should read this policy with a guardian and use the game with the guardian's consent. Children under 14 should use the game only after obtaining guardian consent. We do not actively collect age information. A guardian who believes a child's information was processed without consent may contact us so we can handle it as required by law. The game has no accounts and no stranger-facing posting, messaging or voice chat.

10. Updates

We will provide reasonable in-game notice when this policy changes. We will ask for consent again where the purposes, methods or types of personal information processing change, or where applicable law otherwise requires renewed consent. The current version is available in Settings > Privacy & Data and at https://www.starhalobj.com/games/galaxy-tycoon/privacy/taptap-en.html.
