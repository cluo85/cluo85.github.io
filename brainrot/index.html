Gameplay 玩法介绍

Not Just Meme Fighters — A Hardcore Auto-Battle with Real Depth 不只是看 Meme 角色打架——这款自走棋卡牌游戏的策略深度，可能会让你重新思考"自动战斗"

120+ Brainrot cards, trigger-driven skills, layered buffs — beneath the absurdity lies a genuinely competitive strategy core. 120+ Brainrot 角色卡牌、触发器驱动的技能系统、多层次的 Buff 叠加——在荒诞的外表下，藏着一颗硬核策略的心。

Brainrot Friends looks like a chaotic internet-character brawl, but don't let the ridiculous names fool you. Once you enter combat, you'll quickly realize this is a game of precision and planning — and "auto-battle" never means "sit back and watch." 《网络梗大乱斗》看起来像是一场无厘头的互联网角色大乱斗，但别被那些喊着奇怪名字的 Brainrot 战士骗了。当你真正进入战斗，很快会发现：这是一场需要精密计算的策略博弈，而"自动战斗"并不意味着你只需要袖手旁观。

Two Phases of Combat 战斗怎么打？

Each round has two core phases — Shop first, then Battle. 每场对局分为两个核心阶段——先商店，后战斗。

Shop 商店

You start every round with a fixed budget of 10 gold. Spend it to buy new cards, upgrade existing units, or pivot your composition. Cards have no "high-cost" or "low-cost" tier; power comes entirely from skill trigger synergy. Stack offensive buffs for an alpha strike, or build a defensive counter-combo? Every shop refresh forces a fresh decision. 每回合你拥有固定的10个金币，用这些资源购买新卡牌、升级已有角色、或调整阵容构成。卡牌没有"高费""低费"之分，强弱完全取决于技能触发器的搭配——是堆砌攻击 Buff 一波带走，还是走防御反击流拖到后期？每次商店刷新都在逼迫你做出当下的最优解。

Battle 战斗

Your squad clashes with the enemy automatically. You don't tap every attack — but victory is decided long before the first hit lands. Which cards you picked, how you positioned them, and how their skills chain together — these are the real battle. 你的战队与敌方自动交锋。你不需要手动操作每一次攻击，但胜负早在战斗开始前就已注定：你选择了哪几张卡牌、如何排列站位、技能之间如何触发连携——这些决策才是战斗真正的主体。

Where the Depth Comes From: Skill Types 策略深度从哪来？看几种技能类型就懂了

The trigger-driven skill system is the skeleton of the strategy. Each card's ability is not a simple "press to cast" — it's a condition-effect pairing. Reading the rules can feel abstract, but once you see how these skill types play out in practice, the system's appeal becomes immediate. 触发器驱动的技能系统是这款游戏策略内核的骨架。每张卡牌携带的技能并非简单"点击释放"，而是由触发条件和效果组合而成。光看规则可能有点抽象，但当你看到下面这几类技能的实际表现时，立刻就能明白这套系统的乐趣。

Death-Blessing 死亡时增益型

Trigger: On Death 触发：角色阵亡 Effect: Buff a random ally's ATK & HP 效果：给随机队友+攻击力和生命值

The classic "die so others may live." Place these units on the front line to absorb punishment; when they fall, they bequeath their stats to the carries behind them. Higher levels mean a richer inheritance. 最经典的"死得其所"。把这类角色放在前排吸收伤害，阵亡后把遗产留给身后的核心输出。等级越高，留下的遗产越丰厚。

Opening Strike 开场压制型

Trigger: Battle Start 触发：战斗开始时 Effect: Damage random enemy 效果：对随机敌人造成伤害

They scream their own name and rush out the gate, delivering an opening salvo before the enemy can blink. At higher levels they hit 3 targets instead of 1 — a staple in any rush-down composition. 开局就喊着自己的名字冲出去，给敌方来一波"开场问候"。等级提升后从打1个变成打3个，速攻阵容里的常客。

Kamikaze Core 自爆流核心

Trigger: On Death 触发：角色阵亡 Effect: Damage ALL characters 效果：对所有角色造成伤害

High risk, high reward. When they die, they explode — friend and foe alike. Used well, they're a late-game board clear; used poorly, they're a team-wipe machine. Positioning and timing are everything. 这类角色的风险和回报一样高——阵亡时炸全场，包括自己的队友。用好了是残局清场神器，用砸了是自己团灭发动机。站位和时机，缺一不可。

Pain-to-Power 越战越勇型

Trigger: On Taking Damage 触发：受到伤害时 Effect: Gain ATK 效果：+攻击力

For these fighters, getting hit isn't a setback — it's a charge-up. Park them on the front line and watch their attack multiply over several rounds. Pair with healing and they evolve from "meat shield" to "main carry." 挨打对这类角色来说不是削弱，是充能。放在前排承受攻击，几轮下来攻击力翻几倍。配合治疗角色，能从"肉盾"变成"主C"。

Death-Summon 亡语召唤型

Trigger: On Death 触发：角色阵亡 Effect: Summon a powerful minion 效果：召唤强力召唤物

A seemingly weak card that, on death, spawns a high-ATK, high-HP summon with its own special ability. Many comeback matches begin the moment the opponent thinks they've already won. 表面上是一个属性平平的小角色，但阵亡后会蹦出一个高攻高血还带特殊能力的召唤物。很多翻盘局的起点，就是对手以为已经解决了它。

Chain multiple trigger types together and the strategic space explodes: openers soften the line, growth tanks scale into late-game threats, kamikaze units force the board into a bloody endgame, and summoners clean up the scraps. The same mechanic plays completely differently depending on the surrounding roster. 当你把多种触发链条串联起来时，会产生指数级的策略可能性：是让开场角色先压血线，配合成长型前排拖到后期，还是用自爆角色快速进入残局，再让召唤型角色收割？同一类机制，在不同的阵容里完全是两种打法。

Buffs Add Another Dimension Buff 让战局充满变数

The Buff system pushes every strategy above into a higher dimension. You can kit a unit for a one-shot explosion — the Twisted Donut adds +20 damage to its next attack, perfect for assassinating a key target. Or you can play defense — the Frozen Coconut completely ignores a lethal hit, keeping your frontline alive just long enough. Some buffs even rewrite the battlefield — Rocket Chili flings the enemy’s lead unit straight to the back row before combat, letting your damage hit the core. These buffs stack and combine, turning an ordinary card into your roster’s secret weapon. 而 Buff 系统则让上述所有策略再上一个维度。你可以给角色装备一次性爆发手段——扭曲甜甜圈让下一次攻击伤害+20，适合快速斩杀关键目标；也可以走防守路线——冰冻椰子可以完全忽略一次致命攻击，让前排活到你期望它发挥作用的时刻；还有一些 Buff 会改变战场格局——火箭辣椒能在战斗开始前把敌方最前面的角色直接甩到最后面，让你的输出精准命中敌方核心。这些 Buff 可以叠加、组合，把一张普通的卡牌变成你阵容里的秘密武器。

PVE Teaches, PVP Tests PVE 教你思路，PVP 检验真章

The game currently features 120+ Brainrot cards, each with unique skill sets and trigger logic. PVE campaigns are a curated strategy tutorial — every level teaches a new archetype or counter-relationship. One stage floods you with high-attack-speed enemies, teaching you to leverage kamikaze units to thin their numbers quickly. Another features a low-health enemy with a devastating desperation burst, testing whether you can focus-fire the core within three rounds. PVP is the real crucible: you must read opponent formations, predict trigger timing, and adapt to the shifting meta. A team that dominates PVE can be dismantled in seconds by an off-meta counter in ranked play. 游戏目前收录超过 120 张 Brainrot 角色卡牌，每张都有独立的技能组和触发逻辑。PVE 战役更像一场精心设计的策略教学——每一关都在教你一种新的卡组思路或克制关系。比如某一关敌方全是高攻速阵容，就是在教你"用自爆型角色快速削减对方人数"；另一关敌方有强力残血爆发角色，就是在考你"能否在前三回合集火秒掉核心"。PVP 对战则是真正的试炼场。你不仅要理解自己的阵容，还要预判对手的布阵逻辑、针对当前环境调整策略。一套在 PVE 中无往不利的阵容，可能在 PVP 中被某个冷门 Counter 卡组瞬间瓦解。

Why "Auto-Battle" ≠ "No Control" 为什么"自动战斗"不等于"没有操作"？

The common misconception is that if you can't micromanage combat, strategy doesn't matter. Brainrot Friends flips that logic: because you cannot adjust mid-fight, every pre-fight decision becomes more important. What triggers does the enemy have? Will your buffs outlast their first burst? Can your key damage dealer survive long enough to activate? There is no answer key. Every match is a new puzzle. 很多玩家对自动战斗游戏的误解是："战斗过程不可控，所以策略性弱。"《网络梗大乱斗》的答案是：战斗的不可控，恰恰让"战前决策"变得更加重要。因为你无法在战斗中临时改变策略，所以每一步预判都要提前算好。敌方阵容可能有哪些触发技能？你的 Buff 覆盖能否撑过对方的第一波爆发？你的核心输出角色是否能活到触发条件满足的那一刻？这些问题没有标准答案。每一局对战，都是一次新的解谜。

If you're tired of card games that "look complex but only ask you to stack bigger numbers," Brainrot Friends might restore your faith in what strategy games can be — except this time, your commander is a screaming Brainrot warrior with an unpronounceable name. 如果你厌倦了那些"看起来复杂，实际上只需要堆数值"的卡牌游戏，《网络梗大乱斗》可能会让你找回策略游戏应有的深度——只不过这一次，你的指挥官是一群喊着奇怪名字的 Brainrot 战士。

Team & Story 团队与故事

We're Two Dragon Ball Kids Who Turned Internet Brainrot into Card-Battle Warriors 我们是两个看《龙珠》长大的开发者，把互联网上的"Brainrot"角色变成了卡牌战士

We fused 90s cel-shaded anime aesthetics with the viral meme culture we live in every day. The result? A ridiculous yet genuinely competitive strategy brawl. 我们把从小痴迷的90年代赛璐珞动画美学，和每天浸泡其中的病毒式Meme文化融合到了一起。结果？一场无厘头又热血的策略大乱斗。

Have you ever stumbled across a comment-thread debate where two completely unrelated internet characters are being discussed as if they were heavyweight fighters? We didn't just see those arguments — we decided to build the arena. 你有没有在短视频评论区看到过这样的争论：两个看似毫无关联的互联网角色，网友却在认真讨论"谁更厉害"？我们不仅看到过，还决定做点什么——我们真的搭了一个擂台。

"We grew up on Dragon Ball," we say. "Those hard-edged lines, the saturated cel-shaded colors — they're burned into our brains." Our illustrator can still draw every main and supporting character from memory, no reference needed. So when he first encountered the rising wave of Brainrot characters — those absurd, meme-drenched figures with unpronounceable names — we immediately asked ourselves: what if we reimagined these internet-born creatures through a 90s anime lens, and actually let them fight? "我们从小看《龙珠》长大，那种线条硬朗、色彩浓烈的赛璐珞风格深深印在脑子里。"我们的插画师甚至可以在不看任何参考图的情况下，画出《龙珠》里的每一位主角和配角。而当他第一次看到互联网上兴起的 Brainrot 角色——那些名字古怪、形象夸张、自带 Meme 光环的存在时，我们立刻问自己：如果用90年代动画的美学重新诠释这些角色，让他们在战场上真正打起来，会怎样？

That question became Brainrot Friends — an auto-battle card strategy game where you collect and field 120+ cards across PVE campaigns and real-time PVP. But the detail we want you to remember isn't a mechanic; it's the sound. 这个问题最终变成了《网络梗大乱斗》——一款融合自走棋与卡牌策略的自动战斗游戏。你会收集超过120张卡牌，组建属于自己的战队，在PVE战役中与AI较量策略深度，也可以在PVP模式中与其他玩家一较高下。但我们最希望你记住的，不是某个机制，而是声音。

Every character carries an Italian-flavored name, and when they unleash their ultimate ability, they scream it at full volume. 每个角色的名字都带有意大利语风情的读音，而当他们释放技能时，会大声喊出自己的名字。

"Tralalero Tralala!" "Tralalero Tralala！"

Hearing those ridiculous, rhythmically infectious shouts echo across the battlefield is almost impossible not to laugh at. It's not a bug. It's the soul of Brainrot: these characters were born from the internet's collective absurdity, and rendering that absurdity through the earnest, high-energy grammar of 90s shonen anime is exactly where we think the game's charm lives. 当你听到战场上此起彼伏地响起这些无厘头又充满节奏感的喊声时，很难不笑出声。这不是配音失误，而是我们刻意保留的"Brainrot灵魂"：这些角色从互联网上诞生，天生就带着一种荒诞的幽默感。而用90年代热血动画的方式去演绎这种荒诞，恰恰是我们觉得最迷人的化学反应。

Beneath the surface chaos, we spent a lot of time refining a genuinely deep combat system. Skill combos, trigger windows, buff stacking — every decision rewards precision. The game looks like a meme; it plays like a chess match at 2× speed. 我们花了很多时间打磨战斗系统的策略深度。每张卡牌的技能组合、触发时机、Buff叠加都需要精密计算——表面的无厘头之下，藏着的是硬核的策略对抗。这款游戏看起来像梗，玩起来却像二倍速的国际象棋。

Whether you're here for the nostalgic visual language of 90s animation, drawn in by the magnetic weirdness of Brainrot culture, or simply looking for a strategy game that's hilarious to watch and demanding to master — we built Brainrot Friends to be that answer. 无论你是为了重温90年代动画的视觉记忆而来，还是被 Brainrot 文化的荒诞魅力吸引，抑或只是想找一款"看起来搞笑，玩起来烧脑"的策略游戏——我们就是为了做出这样的游戏，才开发了《网络梗大乱斗》。

Download Brainrot Friends. Pick the most ridiculous card on the roster. Let it scream its own name — then win the fight. 下载《网络梗大乱斗》，选一张最离谱的卡牌，听它喊出自己名字——然后赢下这场战斗。
